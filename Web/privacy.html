DogmaBot

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

DogmaBot is an unofficial, open-source Discord bot that calculates EVE Online ship fits, together with a fit page that shows a fit's full stats in the browser. This policy explains what data the bot and the page handle, where it goes and how you can remove it. The source code is public on GitHub, so every statement below can be checked.

In short

  • DogmaBot has no database. It does not store your messages, your fits or anything about you.
  • A fit is calculated the moment you send it, the bot answers, and nothing is kept afterwards.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies.
  • The developer does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Data the bot receives

The bot reads nothing in your server on its own: it does not read messages and does not request access to message content. It only receives what Discord sends when someone uses one of its commands:

  • /fit and /compare: the fit text typed into the command's form;
  • Analyze fit (the message context menu): the text of the one message the command was used on;
  • with every command, the data Discord attaches to it: the IDs of the user, server and channel, the user's name, and a token that lets the bot reply.

The bot uses this data only to calculate the fit and post the reply. It is processed in memory and discarded once the reply is sent. The reply contains the fit's stats, the ship's image and a link to the fit page with the fit in the link itself; replies to /fit, /compare and Analyze fit are visible in the channel like any other message, and error messages are visible only to you.

Logs

The bot writes technical logs: start-up, connection to Discord and errors. They do not record the text of fits or messages. The logs are kept by the hosting provider that runs the bot, for the period that provider keeps logs, and are used only to keep the bot working.

The fit page

The fit page is a static page served by GitHub Pages. The fit travels in the part of the link after #, which browsers do not send to any server, and the calculation runs entirely in your browser. To show the page, your browser also contacts:

  • GitHub Pages, which serves the page and its data files (GitHub's privacy statement);
  • CCP hf.: images.evetech.net for ship and item images, which reveals which items are in the fit, and esi.evetech.net for average market prices, a request that contains no fit data (CCP's privacy policy);
  • Google Fonts for the page's fonts (Google's privacy policy);
  • jsDelivr for a script that runs the calculator (jsDelivr's privacy policy).

Like any web request, these reveal your IP address and browser details to the service contacted. The page sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The copy buttons put the fit on your clipboard and send it nowhere.

Discord

Commands and replies pass through Discord and are subject to Discord's privacy policy. The bot's replies stay in the channel until someone deletes them; you can delete messages you are allowed to delete, and server moderators can delete any of the bot's replies.

Children

DogmaBot is not directed at children and is meant for people who may use Discord under its terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. The history of every change is public in the repository.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: open an issue at github.com/artem-shimanski/DogmaBot/issues.
