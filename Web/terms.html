The service

DogmaBot calculates the stats of EVE Online ship fits: /fit and the Analyze fit message command show a fit's stats, /compare compares two fits, and the fit page shows a fit in full in the browser. DogmaBot is free, has no accounts and no paid features. It is an unofficial fan project developed in spare time.

Calculations

Stats are calculated by the open-source DogmaKit engine from the game's static data, assuming all skills at level V, raw damage without application and the drones and fighters the ship can launch. They are estimates for theorycrafting and may differ from the game. Market prices on the fit page come from CCP's average prices and are approximate. Do not rely on DogmaBot for decisions where an error would cost you, in game or otherwise.
